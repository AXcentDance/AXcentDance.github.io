# What you will learn — one syllabus per course

This is the editable master list for the **What you will learn** section of every course page. Change anything here, tell me the file is updated, and I will write it into the live pages (English and German together — the German pages are translations of exactly this content).

## How to edit

- **Paragraph above the cards** is the sentence right under the heading.
- **Items** are the numbered cards. Add, delete, reorder, or rewrite them freely — the layout handles three to six per page; do not number them yourself, I renumber.
- Each item is `**Name** — description`. Keep the name short (two to four words) and the description to one sentence; longer names break the two-column layout on mobile.
- **Paragraph below the cards** is the small line that closes the section.
- Leave anything you are happy with untouched.

**Items:**

- **Media** — The half turn that opens most Bachata figures, led and followed cleanly from the very first week.
- **Completo** — The full turn that grows out of the media, with the timing and connection that keep it smooth.
- **Partnerwork vocabulary** — The shared vocabulary of holds, hand positions, and leads that every figure above is assembled from.
- **Hammerlock** — Your first arm position behind the back, prepared safely and released without pulling.
- **Manton** — The shawl figure that wraps you into reverse shadow position and out again with the connection intact.
- **Footwork** — Simple footwork patterns that place your weight correctly and keep you on the beat.

## Bachata Beginner 2

`bachata-beginner-2.html` · Curriculum only, not on the timetable · section heading: *What you will learn*

**Paragraph above the cards:** Bachata is built on 10 fundamental techniques of leading and following, taught across the beginner curriculum in your first year of dance classes — Beginner 2 and 3 then open the door to Bachata Sensual.

**Items:**

- **Impulse energy** — Leading with a short impulse that your partner carries through the movement, instead of steering with the arms.
- **Bodyroll** — Your first sensual body movement — a wave through chest, ribs, and hips, drilled solo before it enters partnerwork.
- **Partnerwork tension** — The elastic tension between partners that makes a lead readable without any force.
- **Cuddle and hammerlock positions** — Two positions you will meet on every social floor, entered and left safely with the connection intact.
- **Dominican footwork** — Traditional Dominican footwork that trains your rhythm and gives you something to play with inside a song.

**Paragraph below the cards:** Beginner 2 is where Bachata Sensual begins — from here, Beginner 3 adds the caída, torsion, and pendulum energy.

## Bachata Beginner 3

`bachata-beginner-3.html` · Tuesdays 19:30 · section heading: *What you will learn*

**Paragraph above the cards:** Bachata is built on 10 fundamental techniques of leading and following, taught across the beginner curriculum in your first year of dance classes — Beginner 2 and 3 then open the door to Bachata Sensual.

**Items:**

- **Diagonal and torsion steps** — Steps that travel on the diagonal and turn through the body — the ground that sensual movement stands on.
- **Caída (dip)** — The dip, prepared and supported so both partners stay in control from the entry to the way out.
- **Pendulum energy** — The swinging energy that carries a movement from one side to the other and back, led without force.
- **Contra-rompe techniques** — The rompe led against your partner’s direction — contrast and control that open a new family of figures.
- **Partnerwork combining fundamentals** — Figures that chain the fundamentals you now own into one continuous dance.

**Paragraph below the cards:** Beginner 3 is the bridge into the Sensual track — finish it, and Bachata Sensual Foundation is your next room.

## Bachata Sensual Foundation

`bachata-sensual-foundation.html` · Wednesdays 19:30 · section heading: *What you will learn*

**Paragraph above the cards:** Bachata Sensual is structured around the 10 Fundamental Techniques of Body Movements. This course rotates through all of them — the cycle repeats, so any week is a valid entry point.

**Items:**

- **10 Fundamental Techniques of Bachata Sensual** — The core vocabulary of Bachata Sensual, taught with a strong emphasis on safety and clean execution.
- **Body waves & isolations** — The movement quality that makes the style — waves, isolations, and circular movement, drilled solo before they enter partnerwork.
- **Frame & connection** — How leading and following actually communicate — a frame that protects both partners and makes every movement feel smooth.
- **Foundational partnerwork** — Each technique placed into real partnerwork you will meet on every social dance floor.
- **Different energies in Bachata** — The energies a movement can be led with — pendulum, impulse, and tension — and how each one changes the same technique.
- **Musicality resources** — Resources for dancing to the song — recognising its sections and instruments and choosing movement that fits them.

**Paragraph below the cards:** These techniques are required for the higher levels and are explained in detail only here — which is why many experienced dancers return to Foundation to review.

**Paragraph above the cards:** Six threads, woven through the block — the step from knowing the techniques to dancing them.

**Items:**

- **The Sensual basic** — The basic that carries the style — grounded, controlled, and unmistakably Sensual.

- **Musicality & breaks** — Hearing the structure of the song — and dancing the breaks instead of dancing through them.
