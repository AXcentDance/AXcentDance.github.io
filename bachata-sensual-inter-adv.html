What you will learn

Five fronts, pushed at a faster pace — for dancers already fluent in the fundamentals.

  1. 01

    Advanced body movement

    Non-fundamental body movements — vocabulary beyond the core ten, drawn from various styles.

  2. 02

    Musicality & interpretation

    Reading the song and interpreting it — dancing shaped by the music instead of the pattern.

  3. 03

    Technical rueda

    Rueda drills that sharpen technique, reaction, and connection with every partner in the circle.

  4. 04

    Improvisation tools

    Tools to leave choreography behind and build your own dance in the moment.

  5. 05

    Timing & dynamics

    Playing with timing and dynamics — contrast, suspension, and release.

The pace is faster here: Foundation and Improver concepts are not reviewed — fluency in the 10 fundamentals is assumed.

Is this your class?

How a class works

Sixty minutes, one idea, many partners.

  1. 01
    Warm up & connect

    Arrive, settle in, and get the body moving at the level the hour demands.

  2. 02
    The idea of the week

    One advanced pattern or concept, at full pace — no basics review.

  3. 03
    Into partnerwork

    The material danced with musicality, improvisation, and your own interpretation.

  4. 04
    Rotate & dance

    Partners rotate through the class — you leave having danced with everyone.

Good questions

How much experience do I need?+

Attendance of the Improver level and at least 2 years of regular dancing. You must be fluent in the 10 fundamental techniques of Bachata Sensual — we do not spend time explaining these basics in this course.

What is the difference between 19:30 and 20:30?+

Both Thursday groups work at the Intermediate / Advanced level; the 20:30 group is the most advanced room in the school and joins upon instructor approval. Ask us and we will place you honestly.

Can I join in the middle of a block?+

Ask us before you join mid-block — we will tell you honestly whether the current week is a good entry point. The trial class is free either way.

Do I need a partner?+

No. Partners rotate throughout the class, so you dance with everyone — most people come alone.

What if I miss a week?+

If you are ill, you can make up one missed class per term in another suitable course. The full policy is in our terms.
