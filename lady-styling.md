# Lady Styling

Styling is a skill of body awareness. This course teaches your body the correct alignment and mechanics to style effortlessly, in solo moments and in partnerwork. Arm styling is only the tip of the iceberg; it is about self-expression and confidence.

## What you will learn

Five layers of styling, built from the ground up, because it is your body, not your arms, that does the styling.

- Arm styling technique Arm styling that flows from the body: placement, pathways, and intention behind every line.

- Body isolations & waves The isolations and waves that give your styling its texture and follow the energy of the music.

- Footwork & shines Footwork and shines that hold their own in any solo moment on the dance floor.

- Spinning technique Clean, balanced spins, prepared so they feel stable at any tempo.

- Confidence & expression The part that turns technique into presence: self-expression you can take onto any dance floor.

Everything here feeds straight back into your partner dancing, whichever level of the ladder you dance in.

## How a class works

Sixty minutes, one styling layer, the whole room dancing.

- 01 Warm up & body prep Mobilise the body: the alignment and mechanics that styling is built on.

- 02 The technique of the week One styling element, broken down slowly: mechanics before aesthetics.

- 03 Into the combination The element inside a short combination, built up phrase by phrase.

- 04 Drill it to music The combination, full out, as a group. No partner needed at any point.

## Good questions

Do I need a partner?+

No, and here not even a rotating one. Lady Styling is danced solo from start to finish.

How much experience do I need?+

Around 1 year of dancing. The course is open level beyond that: every element is broken down from the mechanics up, so dancers from every level of the ladder train side by side.

Can I join in the middle of a block?+

Ask us before you join mid-block. We will tell you honestly whether the current week is a good entry point. The trial class is free either way.

What should I wear?+

Comfortable clothes you can move in and shoes you can spin in. Clean sneakers are perfect. Heels are welcome once your technique is ready, never required. Our [what-to-wear guide](https://axcentdance.com/blog/what-to-wear-bachata) has the details.

What if I miss a week?+

You can declare up to two planned absences at registration and one sick day per pass, and we extend your pass by that class day. The full policy is in our [terms](https://axcentdance.com/terms).
