Act I — Zurich, our studio 17 – 19 October 2026

Dominican Bachata Bootcamp

For one weekend in October, the studio in Altstetten runs on Dominican time. Seven hours of partnerwork-focused training with Aitor Gomez and Sarah La Morena — leading, following, and the connection that no video tutorial can give you.

Come alone or bring your crew. By Sunday evening you will have danced with everyone in the room.

We travel as a group Act II — Milan, together 20 – 22 November 2026

Milano Sensual Congress 2026

Every November we pack our dance shoes and cross the Alps. The Milano Sensual Congress is one of Italy's most prestigious Bachata Sensual events — three days of workshops, shows, and social dancing with world-class artists.

You do not have to organize anything alone: travel with the Zurich group and arrive with friends already at your side.

Where
Milan, Italy
Artists
