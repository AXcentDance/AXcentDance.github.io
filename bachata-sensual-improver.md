## What you will learn

Six threads, woven through the block: the step from knowing the techniques to dancing them.

- Variations of the 10 fundamentals The Foundation vocabulary, expanded: every core technique with new variations to draw from.

- The Sensual basic The basic that carries the style: grounded, controlled, and unmistakably Sensual.

- Transitions between fundamentals Fluent transitions that connect the techniques into one continuous dance instead of a sequence of moves.

- More advanced body movement Body movement beyond the basics, layered carefully onto the fundamentals you already own.

- Musicality & breaks Hearing the structure of the song, and dancing the breaks instead of dancing through them.

- Leading & following dynamics The finer dynamics between partners that make complex combinations feel effortless on both sides.

Improver assumes the 10 fundamental techniques from [Sensual Foundation](https://axcentdance.com/bachata-sensual-foundation). If they are new to you, that room comes first.

## Good questions

How much experience do I need?+

Around 2 years of dance experience. You should be familiar with the patterns of Bachata Beginner 0 and 1, the foundational body movements (the wave and the contra) and the core [Bachata Sensual Foundation](https://axcentdance.com/bachata-sensual-foundation) techniques.

Can I join in the middle of a block?+

Ask us before you join mid-block. We will tell you honestly whether the current week is a good entry point. The trial class is free either way.

Do I need a partner?+

No. Partners rotate throughout the class, so you dance with everyone. Most people come alone.

What should I wear?+

Comfortable clothes you can move in and shoes that let you turn. Clean sneakers are perfect. Our [what-to-wear guide](https://axcentdance.com/blog/what-to-wear-bachata) has the details.

What if I miss a week?+

You can declare up to two planned absences at registration and one sick day per pass, and we extend your pass by that class day. The full policy is in our [terms](https://axcentdance.com/terms).
