Meet your instructors

Two artists, one weekend

Seven hours split across three days: two hours of partnerwork on Friday, three solo hours with Sarah and Aitor on Saturday, and two solo hours with Aitor on Sunday: the technique underneath the dance, and how it feels with a real partner in your arms.

All three days with

Aitor Gomez

Friday partnerwork · Saturday & Sunday solo technique

Aitor teaches on all three days: partnerwork on Friday evening, the Saturday solo block together with Sarah, and two closing solo hours on Sunday: footwork, syncopation, and the timing underneath Dominican Bachata.

Friday & Saturday with

Sarah La Morena

Friday partnerwork · Saturday solo body movement & musicality

Who this weekend is for

This bootcamp is designed for dancers with at least one year of Bachata experience. It is not a beginner event. It is the deep end, and that is the point.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it only Dominican Bachata? +
Yes! This event focuses exclusively on authentic Dominican Bachata technique, musicality, and footwork.
Is it for every level? +
No, this bootcamp is designed for dancers with at least 1 year of Bachata experience. It is not suitable for absolute beginners.
Can I resell my ticket? +
Yes, you can transfer your ticket to another person. Please email us with the new attendee's name so we can update our guest list.

Not there yet?

Build your first year with us: weekly classes, patient teachers, and a free first lesson. The next bootcamp will still be here when you are ready.
