Meet your instructors

Two artists, one weekend

Seven hours split into two focused blocks, both built around partnerwork: how you lead it, how you follow it, and how it feels to actually dance it with a real partner in your arms.

Block I — 4 hours with

Aitor Gomez

4 hours · Partnerwork & leading technique

Aitor's four hours are built around partnerwork: how to lead with clarity, how your frame changes what your partner feels, and the footwork and syncopation that only make sense once someone is in front of you to lead through it.

Press play. This is the connection you will be training.

Aitor on Instagram
Block II — 3 hours with

Sarah La Morena

3 hours · Partnerwork & following technique

Sarah's three hours turn Aitor's technique into a real partnership: how to follow, how to answer a lead instead of guessing it, and the body movement and musicality that make two people dancing look like one idea.

Too often at congresses you pay a premium for a brief glimpse of the top artists. We built this weekend the other way around: fewer people, more hours, and time to actually dance with a partner, not just watch one.

Sarah on Instagram

Who this weekend is for

This bootcamp is designed for dancers with at least one year of Bachata experience. It is not a beginner event — it is the deep end, and that is the point.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it only Dominican Bachata? +
Yes! This event focuses exclusively on authentic Dominican Bachata technique, musicality, and footwork.
Is it for every level? +
No, this bootcamp is designed for dancers with at least 1 year of Bachata experience. It is not suitable for absolute beginners.
Can I resell my ticket? +
Yes, you can transfer your ticket to another person. Please email us with the new attendee's name so we can update our guest list.
