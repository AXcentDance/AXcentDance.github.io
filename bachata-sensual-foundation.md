## What you will learn

Bachata Sensual is structured around the 10 Fundamental Techniques of Body Movements. This course rotates through all of them. The cycle repeats, so any week is a valid entry point.

- 10 Fundamental Techniques of Bachata Sensual The core vocabulary of Bachata Sensual, taught with a strong emphasis on safety and clean execution.

- Body waves & isolations The movement quality that makes the style: waves, isolations, and circular movement, drilled solo before they enter partnerwork.

- Frame & connection How leading and following actually communicate: a frame that protects both partners and makes every movement feel smooth.

- Foundational partnerwork Each technique placed into real partnerwork you will meet on every social dance floor.

- Different energies in Bachata The energies a movement can be led with (pendulum, impulse, and tension) and how each one changes the same technique.

- Musicality resources Resources for dancing to the song: recognising its sections and instruments and choosing movement that fits them.

These techniques are required for the higher levels and are explained in detail only here, which is why many experienced dancers return to Foundation to review.

## Good questions

Can I join in the middle of a block?+

Yes. The syllabus rotates through the 10 fundamentals, so any week is a valid entry point. Book a free trial and start whenever suits you.

Do I need a partner?+

No. Partners rotate throughout the class, so you dance with everyone. Most people come alone.

What should I wear?+

Comfortable clothes you can move in and shoes that let you turn. Clean sneakers are perfect. Our [what-to-wear guide](https://axcentdance.com/blog/what-to-wear-bachata) has the details.

What if I miss a week?+

You can declare up to two planned absences at registration and one sick day per pass, and we extend your pass by that class day. The full policy is in our [terms](https://axcentdance.com/terms).
