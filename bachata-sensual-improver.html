What you will learn

Six threads, woven through the block — the step from knowing the techniques to dancing them.

  1. 01

    Variations of the 10 fundamentals

    The Foundation vocabulary, expanded — every core technique with new variations to draw from.

  2. 02

    The Sensual basic

    The basic that carries the style — grounded, controlled, and unmistakably Sensual.

  3. 03

    Transitions between fundamentals

    Fluent transitions that connect the techniques into one continuous dance instead of a sequence of moves.

  4. 04

    More advanced body movement

    Body movement beyond the basics, layered carefully onto the fundamentals you already own.

  5. 05

    Musicality & breaks

    Hearing the structure of the song — and dancing the breaks instead of dancing through them.

  6. 06

    Leading & following dynamics

    The finer dynamics between partners that make complex combinations feel effortless on both sides.

Improver assumes the 10 fundamental techniques from Sensual Foundation — if they are new to you, that room comes first.

How a class works

Sixty minutes, one variation, many partners.

  1. 01
    Warm up & review

    Arrive, settle in, and revisit what the room learned last week.

  2. 02
    The variation of the week

    One variation or transition, broken down solo — quality of movement first.

  3. 03
    Into partnerwork

    The same material inside a combination, danced to the phrasing of the music.

  4. 04
    Rotate & dance

    Partners rotate through the class — you leave having danced with everyone.

Good questions

How much experience do I need?+

Around 2 years of dance experience. You should be familiar with the patterns of Bachata Beginner 0 and 1, the foundational body movements — the wave and the contra — and the core Bachata Sensual Foundation techniques.

Can I join in the middle of a block?+

Ask us before you join mid-block — we will tell you honestly whether the current week is a good entry point. The trial class is free either way.

Do I need a partner?+

No. Partners rotate throughout the class, so you dance with everyone — most people come alone.

What should I wear?+

Comfortable clothes you can move in and shoes that let you turn — clean sneakers are perfect. Our what-to-wear guide has the details.

What if I miss a week?+

If you are ill, you can make up one missed class per term in another suitable course. The full policy is in our terms.
