## What you will learn

Bachata is built on 10 fundamental techniques of leading and following, taught across the beginner curriculum in your first year of dance classes. Beginner 2 and 3 then open the door to Bachata Sensual.

- Media The half turn that opens most Bachata figures, led and followed cleanly from the very first week.

- Completo The full turn that grows out of the media, with the timing and connection that keep it smooth.

- Partnerwork vocabulary The shared vocabulary of holds, hand positions, and leads that every figure above is assembled from.

- Hammerlock Your first arm position behind the back, prepared safely and released without pulling.

- Manton The shawl figure that wraps you into reverse shadow position and out again with the connection intact.

- Footwork Simple footwork patterns that place your weight correctly and keep you on the beat.

## How a class works

Sixty minutes, one step, many partners.

- 01 Warm up & review Shake off the day, settle in, and revisit the steps from last week.

- 02 The step of the week One new building block, broken down slowly. Feet first, then timing.

- 03 Into partnerwork The same step danced in couples, at a pace where nobody is left behind.

- 04 Rotate & dance Partners rotate through the class, so you leave having danced with everyone.

## Good questions

Do I need any experience?+

No. A simple turn, the media, and basic partnerwork are helpful, and all of them are taught step by step if you arrive without them. Absolute first-timers can also start with Bachata Beginner 0 on Mondays at 19:30; see [the Beginner 0 page](https://axcentdance.com/bachata-beginner-0).

When does Beginner 1 run?+

Not this season. We open a level when there are enough dancers sitting at it, so the honest answer is that it depends on demand. Tell us you want it. That is how the room gets built, and in the meantime [Beginner 0](https://axcentdance.com/bachata-beginner-0) covers the same ground from the first step.

Do I need a partner?+

No. Partners rotate throughout the class, so you dance with everyone. Most people come alone.

What should I wear?+

Comfortable clothes you can move in and shoes that let you turn. Clean sneakers are perfect. Coming straight from work? A change of shirt, deodorant, and mints make the hour nicer for everyone. Our [what-to-wear guide](https://axcentdance.com/blog/what-to-wear-bachata) has the details.

What if I miss a week?+

You can declare up to two planned absences at registration and one sick day per pass, and we extend your pass by that class day. The full policy is in our [terms](https://axcentdance.com/terms).
