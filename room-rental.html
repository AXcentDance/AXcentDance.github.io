Frequently Asked Questions

Can I rent the room for private parties? +
Yes, our studio is perfect for birthdays, workshops, and private events. One-time bookings for business purposes start at CHF 50; contact us for availability and a customized quote for parties and special events.
Is there parking available? +
Yes, there is street parking available on the same street as the school. Public transport (Tram 2, Bus 31) is just 2 minutes walking distance.
What is your cancellation policy? +
Plans change, and that is okay. Cancel at least 1 week before your booking and you will receive a full refund. Cancel between 1 week and 48 hours before, and 50% of the rate applies. Cancellations within the last 48 hours are unfortunately non-refundable, as the room can no longer be offered to another booking at such short notice.
