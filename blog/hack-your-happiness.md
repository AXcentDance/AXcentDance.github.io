# How to Act on Your Happiness This Zurich Spring: The Science-Backed Way to Bloom

By Ale & Xidan · AXcent Dance · [Meet your teachers](https://axcentdance.com/about)

As the Zurich lakeside bursts into blossom and the days stretch longer, there is a palpable sense of renewal in the air. But spring isn't just about watching the flowers grow. It is about deciding to act on your happiness and unleash the energy you've been storing all winter.

Science shows that the transition into spring is the perfect time to reset your neural pathways. By combining the natural increase in sunlight with rhythmic movement and social connection, you can amplify your "Spring Awakening." At AXcent Dance, we believe that happiness isn't a passive state; it is a choice you make every time you step onto the dance floor. ## 3 Ways to Act on Your Happiness This Season

☀️

### The Serotonin Surge

Spring sunlight and physical movement work together to skyrocket your serotonin levels. Dancing Bachata adds an extra layer of joy, ensuring you feel energized throughout the week.

🌸

### Community in Bloom

Just as Zurich blossoms, our community thrives in the spring energy. Meet new people, share a laugh, and connect with a diverse group of positive-minded individuals. No partner needed!

⚡

### Revitalize Your Body

Shake off the winter stagnation. Learning the fluid movements of Bachata Sensual is the ultimate way to reconnect with your body and feel agile, confident, and alive.

## The "Social Shortcut" to a Vibrant Life

Integrating into a new social circle in Zurich can sometimes feel slow, but dancing acts as a "social shortcut." The synchronized movement releases bonding hormones faster than any other activity, making it the perfect way to build lasting friendships this spring.

It is time to stop waiting for happiness to find you and start acting on it. We recommend reading our [Beginner Guide](https://axcentdance.com/beginner-guide) to see how easy it is to start your journey.

💡

### Did you know?

Spring is the peak time for neuroplasticity, the brain's ability to form new connections. Learning a new skill like dance right now has long-term benefits for your cognitive health and mood.
