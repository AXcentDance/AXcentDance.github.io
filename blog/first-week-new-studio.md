# First Week Magic: Celebrating Our New Home in Zurich

By Ale & Xidan · AXcent Dance · [Meet your teachers](https://axcentdance.com/about)

What a week! We are still buzzing from the incredible energy you all brought to our brand-new studio in Zurich Altstetten. Closing our first week of classes in this beautiful new space feels like a major milestone, and we could not have done it without our amazing community.

## A Heartfelt Thank You

To everyone who joined us for a class, stepped onto the new dance floor for the first time, or simply stopped by to say hello: thank you. Seeing the studio filled with laughter, focus, and passion reminded us exactly why we do what we do. Learn more about [our story and the team behind AXcent Dance](https://axcentdance.com/about).

Moving to a new space is always a significant transition, but you made us feel at home from the very first class. It was a joy to see the studio full of life, and watching everyone connect so naturally on the dance floor was the perfect start to our new chapter.

## The Journey Continues

This is only the beginning of our new chapter at Hermetschloostrasse 73. We have so much planned for this season, and we cannot wait to share more moments of growth and dance with you.

If you have not had a chance to visit the new studio yet, we would love to welcome you. Browse our [weekly class schedule](https://axcentdance.com/schedule) or [register for a course](https://axcentdance.com/registration) to secure your spot.
