# The Complete Latin Dance School Experience

By Ale & Xidan · AXcent Dance · [Meet your teachers](https://axcentdance.com/about)

Many students come to us looking specifically for Bachata classes. However, what they discover is a comprehensive Latin Dance School that offers much more. To truly master the art of movement, we believe a dancer must explore the full spectrum of Latin rhythms.

At AXcent Dance in Zurich, our curriculum is designed to create versatile, confident dancers. We do not just teach steps; we teach music, culture, and connection across multiple disciplines.

## More Than Just Bachata

💃

### Salsa

The heartbeat of Latin dance. Learning Salsa improves your timing, speed, and turn patterns, which directly enhances your Bachata flow.

🥁

### Merengue

The root of it all. Merengue teaches you hip movement (cuban motion) and weight transfer like no other dance.

✨

### ChaCha

Precision and playfulness. ChaCha challenges your rhythm and footwork, adding sharpness to your style.

🔥

### Styling & Body Movement

It is not just what you do, but how you do it. Our styling classes focus on arm lines, isolation, and expression.

## Why Choose a Holistic Latin Dance School?

When you join a specialized Latin Dance School like AXcent Dance, you are not just signing up for a course; you are entering an academy of movement. Discover more about [what Bachata is and why it captivates dancers worldwide](https://axcentdance.com/guide-bachata).

Our instructors, Ale and Xidan, integrate concepts from **Salsa, Mambo, ChaCha**, and classical technique into every **Bachata** class. Although our courses are focused on Bachata, these diverse Latin styles are part of our holistic curriculum to build complete dancers. This cross-disciplinary approach creates dancers who are adaptable, musical, and visually stunning to watch. A true Latin dancer can hear any rhythm, be it a fast Mambo or a slow Bolero, and know exactly how to express it.

Whether you want to shine on the social floor or perform on stage, understanding the wider world of Latin dance is essential. It gives you the tools to improvise and the confidence to dance with anyone, anywhere.
