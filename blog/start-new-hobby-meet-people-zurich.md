# Why Bachata is the Best New Hobby for Meeting People in Zurich

By Ale & Xidan · AXcent Dance · [Meet your teachers](https://axcentdance.com/about)

Moving to a new city, or just feeling stuck in a routine? It is incredibly common to want to start a new hobby and meet new people in Zurich. If you are looking for an exciting way to socialize while actually trying something new, we have the perfect answer.

Bachata is more than just a dance; it is an international community that is incredibly welcoming and vibrant right here in Switzerland. Let us show you why taking a [Bachata class](https://axcentdance.com/schedule) is the ultimate social experience for anyone looking for a fresh start. Whether you need a [beginner guide](https://axcentdance.com/beginner-guide) or want to jump straight into the action, you will find a home with us. Still comparing options? See our full list of [hobbies to meet people in Zurich](https://axcentdance.com/blog/hobbies-to-meet-people-zurich).

## 3 Reasons to Choose Bachata as Your New Hobby

🤝

### Built-in Community

In our classes, we rotate partners constantly. You will naturally interact with everyone in the room. It is the easiest, most pressure-free way to meet dozens of new people in an hour.

💪

### Physical & Mental Health

Dancing combines cardiovascular exercise with mental focus, releasing endorphins and completely unplugging you from your daily stress.

🎉

### Amazing Events & Parties

Once you learn a few steps, you can attend social [dance events](https://axcentdance.com/events) around Zurich every week. There is always an incredible party happening.

Our classes are built around connection, joy, and leaving your stress behind.

## Where to Begin?

You do not need previous [dance](https://axcentdance.com/guide-bachata) experience, and you definitely do not need to bring a partner to start this new hobby. Come exactly as you are. Our beginner courses are designed specifically to help you take that brave first step into a studio environment with confidence.

From the classroom directly to the dance floor!
