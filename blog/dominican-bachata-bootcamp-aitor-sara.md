# What to Expect at the Dominican Bachata Bootcamp with Aitor & Sarah

By Ale & Xidan · AXcent Dance · [Meet your teachers](https://axcentdance.com/about)

Zurich, are you ready for the real flavor? We are proud to announce the biggest event of the year for fans of authentic dance: the [Dominican Bachata Bootcamp in Zurich](https://axcentdance.com/dominican-bootcamp). On October 16th, 17th & 18th, 2026, we are bringing two of the world's most exciting artists to AXcent Dance: Aitor Gomez and Sarah La Morena.

Sometimes you need to go back to the roots to find what can bring you to the next level. Dominican Bachata is not just about footwork; it is about musicality, flow, timing, creativity, and that unmistakable groove that makes you want to move. Explore the [full history and roots of Bachata](https://axcentdance.com/blog/roots-of-bachata) to understand the culture behind the movement. And there is nobody better to teach this than Aitor and Sarah.

## Event Highlights

🔥

### 7 Hours Intensive

Forget 1-hour workshops where you learn a pattern and forget it. This is a Bootcamp. 2 hours of partnerwork, 3 solo hours with Sarah and Aitor, 2 solo hours with Aitor. Deep dive into technique.

🇩🇴

### Authentic Culture

Experience the true roots of Bachata. Learn about history, musicality, and the feeling behind the movement.

🎯

### Personal Feedback

Small group setting ensures you get individual attention from the masters themselves. Correct your bad habits on the spot.

## Why Bootcamp Format?

At AXcent Dance, we believe in Deep Learning. Congresses are great for parties, but if you want to actually improve, you need focused time with the instructor. With 7 hours of training, you will have time to master the material. New to Bachata? Start with our [beginner guide](https://axcentdance.com/beginner-guide) and check our [weekly schedule](https://axcentdance.com/schedule) to build your foundation before the bootcamp:

- Understand the fundamental body movement (not just steps).

- Work on musicality and timing.

- Ask questions and get personal feedback.

- Practice the material immediately.
