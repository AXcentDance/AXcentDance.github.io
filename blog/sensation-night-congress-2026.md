# Sensation Night Congress 2026: Join Our Team!

By Ale & Xidan · AXcent Dance · [Meet your teachers](https://axcentdance.com/about)

This weekend isn't just another dancing weekend. It is Sensation Night Congress 2026, and it is going to be legendary! And guess what? AXcent will be there to represent and give a workshop to an international crowd.

But we are not going alone. We are sharing the stage with the absolute giants of the Bachata world. If you want to experience the highest level of dance, connect with our community, and have an unforgettable weekend, you need to be there.
