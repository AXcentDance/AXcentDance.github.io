## Two artists, one weekend

Seven hours split across three days: two hours of partnerwork on Friday, three solo hours with Sarah and Aitor on Saturday, and two solo hours with Aitor on Sunday: the technique underneath the dance, and how it feels with a real partner in your arms.

All three days with

### Aitor Gomez

Friday partnerwork · Saturday & Sunday solo technique

Aitor teaches on all three days: partnerwork on Friday evening, the Saturday solo block together with Sarah, and two closing solo hours on Sunday: footwork, syncopation, and the timing underneath Dominican Bachata.

Press play. This is the connection you will be training.

[Aitor on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/aitorbachata/)

Friday & Saturday with

### Sarah La Morena

Friday partnerwork · Saturday solo body movement & musicality

Sarah opens the weekend with Aitor in Friday's partnerwork, then shares the Saturday solo block: body movement, weight changes, and the musicality that makes Dominican Bachata look effortless.

Too often at congresses you pay a premium for a brief glimpse of the top artists. We built this weekend the other way around: fewer people, more hours, and time to actually dance with a partner, not just watch one.

[Sarah on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/sarah.lamorena/)
