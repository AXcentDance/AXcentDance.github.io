## Good questions

How many lessons will we need? +

Most couples need around eight hours of lessons, or continue until they feel comfortable and confident for the big day. The final price may vary depending on the number of hours and the choreography you choose.

When should we start? +

Ideally three to four months before the wedding, so you can rehearse without stress. A later start is possible; we adapt the plan to the time you have.

Why Bachata as a wedding dance? +

Bachata is naturally romantic and intimate, with warm rhythms and often sensual lyrics about love, trust, and connection. Its close partnership and flowing movements make it a beautiful way to tell your story together while still feeling natural and personal on the dance floor.
