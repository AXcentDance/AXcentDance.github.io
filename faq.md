## Before your first class

Do I need a partner to join? +

No, you do not need a partner! We rotate partners frequently during class. This is the best way to learn how to lead and follow different people, and it is a great way to make new friends. Of course, if you come with a partner and prefer to stay together, that is also allowed.

Yes! All our classes are taught in English to welcome our international community in Zurich. We also speak Italian, and our teachers speak German, if you need extra clarification!

Is there parking available? +

Yes, there are public parking spots available nearby (Blue Zone) and a parking garage at the Letzipark shopping center, which is a short walk away. We are also easily accessible by public transport (Tram 2, Bus 31).

What if I miss a class? +

You can declare up to two planned absences when you register, plus one sick day per pass in the Student Portal before 12:00 on the day you will miss. In each case we extend your pass by that class day, so the lesson is never lost. You can also catch up a missed class by attending a similar level class on a different day within the same course period.
