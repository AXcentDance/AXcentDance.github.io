Lady Styling

Styling is a skill of body awareness — this course teaches your body the correct alignment and mechanics to style effortlessly, in solo moments and in partnerwork. Arm styling is only the tip of the iceberg; it is about self-expression and confidence.

What you will learn

Five layers of styling, built from the ground up — because it is your body, not your arms, that does the styling.

  1. 01

    Arm styling technique

    Arm styling that flows from the body — placement, pathways, and intention behind every line.

  2. 02

    Body isolations & waves

    The isolations and waves that give your styling its texture and follow the energy of the music.

  3. 03

    Footwork & shines

    Footwork and shines that hold their own in any solo moment on the dance floor.

  4. 04

    Spinning technique

    Clean, balanced spins — prepared so they feel stable at any tempo.

  5. 05

    Confidence & expression

    The part that turns technique into presence — self-expression you can take onto any dance floor.

Everything here feeds straight back into your partner dancing — whichever level of the ladder you dance in.

How a class works

Sixty minutes, one styling layer, the whole room dancing.

  1. 01
    Warm up & body prep

    Mobilise the body — the alignment and mechanics that styling is built on.

  2. 02
    The technique of the week

    One styling element, broken down slowly — mechanics before aesthetics.

  3. 03
    Into the combination

    The element inside a short combination, built up phrase by phrase.

  4. 04
    Drill it to music

    The combination, full out, as a group — no partner needed at any point.

Good questions

Do I need a partner?+

No — and here not even a rotating one. Lady Styling is danced solo from start to finish.

How much experience do I need?+

Around 1 year of dancing. The course is open level beyond that — every element is broken down from the mechanics up, so dancers from every level of the ladder train side by side.

Can I join in the middle of a block?+

Ask us before you join mid-block — we will tell you honestly whether the current week is a good entry point. The trial class is free either way.

What should I wear?+

Comfortable clothes you can move in and shoes you can spin in — clean sneakers are perfect. Heels are welcome once your technique is ready, never required. Our what-to-wear guide has the details.

What if I miss a week?+

If you are ill, you can make up one missed class per term in another suitable course. The full policy is in our terms.
